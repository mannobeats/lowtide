# Code of Conduct

Lowtide is made by people who want a better Mac for developers. We want taking part to be a
good experience for everyone, whatever their background, identity or level of experience.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and take criticism of code and ideas, never of people.
- Respect people's time: be clear, and say when you can't follow through.
- Help newcomers find their footing.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or sexual language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and wherever
someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/mannobeats/lowtide/security/advisories/new) or by
contacting a maintainer directly on GitHub. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may
remove, edit or reject contributions, and warn, suspend or ban anyone who breaks this code.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
